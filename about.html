What we record

We read the vendor's product, membership and shipping pages and record the listed prices, sale prices and terms. Percent savings are calculated from the two prices on the page. Shipping and other promises that are marked as the seller's claims are the seller's, not ours.

Status labels

What does Confirmed mean?

Confirmed means we applied the code at checkout, and saw the total change. We use this label only when that happened.

What does Listed by vendor mean?

Listed by vendor means the offer appears on the vendor's own page, such as a sale price or membership discount, and we recorded it. We did not necessarily test it in a cart.

What does Active mean on the coupons page?

Active means the code was listed as working in our records. It is not a guarantee for today. Confirm the total in your cart before you pay.

What does Unconfirmed mean?

Unconfirmed means the code was given to us or appears on the vendor's referral link, but we have not seen it change a total. We still list it, and we say so.

Stale and expired offers

Offers can go stale. If a code fails, treat the offer as ended and confirm it on the vendor's page. Offers we know have ended are removed or marked as expired.

How we make money

Some buttons open the vendor through a referral link, and a commission may be earned at no extra cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure. Commission does not decide which prices we list.

EDITORIAL STANDARDS

What this site is, and what it is not

Peptide Discount is an independent directory of coupon codes from research-peptide vendors, with a price per mg calculator and plain-language guides. It does not sell products, test products or review lab reports. It is not a quality ranking, and nothing on it is medical, dosing or legal advice.

  • No invented data

    We do not make up codes, discounts, prices or vendor details. When a detail is not known, the page says it is not confirmed.

  • Offers as published

    Codes are listed as vendors and their referral programmes publish them. Delivery, testing-lab and COA details are the vendor’s own statements.

  • Neutral ordering

    The coupon table is sorted by discount percentage by default. We do not rank vendors by quality, because we have not tested their products.

  • Clear money disclosure

    Some buttons use referral links and the owner may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure.

  • Honest limits

    A status of Active means the code was listed as working in our records. Vendors change rules without notice, so the cart total is the final check.

  • Corrections

    If a code stops working or a detail is wrong, tell the site owner. We fix errors and remove entries that cannot be supported.

WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM

Sources and limits of the information

Coupon codes come from the vendors’ own promotions and referral programmes. Delivery windows, shipping origin, testing laboratories and COA counts are taken from what each vendor states on its own site. Price per mg figures are arithmetic from prices and vial sizes, and they say nothing about purity or quality.

We have not tested any product and we have not independently verified any lab report. Where a vendor names a laboratory, that is the vendor’s claim, and the certificate for your own lot is the document that matters. The research-use-only label does not make a product safe or lawful for any use, so read our guide on what research use only means.
